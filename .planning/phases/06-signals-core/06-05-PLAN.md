<objective>
Ship the `SignalStore` contract and its `local` driver — the event-history half of the feature, and
the table D-06's idempotency rests on.

Purpose: D-06 makes overlapping and retried flushes harmless **by construction, not by
coordination**. The construction is this: a trail entry keyed on the `hubspot_signals` row it came
from, so re-appending is a no-op. That unique key is the whole mechanism, and it is what lets this
phase ship no claim and no lease — the deduplication-on-identity lesson Phase 5 paid for.

Output: `Signals\Contracts\SignalStore`, `Signals\Stores\LocalSignalStore`, the
`hubspot_signal_trail` migration, the `HUBSPOT_SIGNAL_STORE` resolution with a throwing default arm,
and `ConfigurationException::unknownSignalStore()`.
</objective>
